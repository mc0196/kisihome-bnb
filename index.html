Our Home

A warm and cozy apartment, run with love

Nestled in the quiet hillside hamlet of Vestreno, in the municipality of Valvarrone above the charming town of Dervio, our apartment is a family-run retreat managed by a mother and daughter who take great pride in welcoming guests.

The apartment sleeps up to 4 guests with a comfortable bedroom and a sofa bed in the living area. Bright, well-kept and full of character. Whether you're here to relax by the lake, hike the surrounding hills, or simply slow down. This is your home away from home, and we'll be happy to host you.

Where We Are

Valvarrone, above Lake Como

A hidden gem in the Italian Lake District where mountains meet water

Vestreno is a small, ancient village in the mountains above Dervio, on the eastern branch of Lake Como. From here, you can enjoy breathtaking views over the lake and easy access to all that the area has to offer.

  • 10 min to Dervio town centre & swimmable lake shore
  • 30 min to Lecco by car
  • 1 hr to Milan by train from Dervio
  • 20 min to Varenna ferry terminal, with connections to all lake destinations. Check ferry schedules
  • Trekking & hikes in the surrounding hills. See the trail guide
